Lagos Urban Heat Island, 2019–2029

Four December dry seasons of Landsat 8/9 surface temperature show built-up Lagos running 4.5–5.5 °C hotter than vegetated land, with the heat concentrated in Shomolu, Lagos Mainland, Surulere and Mushin. Includes a hindcast-tested projection to 2029.

About

I'm a Professional Services Analyst at Sambus Geospatial Nigeria Limited in Lagos, where I deploy enterprise ArcGIS solutions and build client demonstrations. Before that I worked in technical sales, and as a research assistant at the Centre of Excellence for Sustainable Environment and Social Inclusion and with the PALM-TREEs Consortium.

My thesis measured greenhouse-gas concentrations across Nigerian states with Sentinel-5P TROPOMI and Google Earth Engine, and became a peer-reviewed paper in Remote Sensing in Earth Systems Sciences (2026). I'm now working on attributing gas-flaring emissions in the Niger Delta, and preparing for a funded Master's in geospatial science.

How I work

  • Open data first: Landsat, Sentinel, VIIRS, OpenStreetMap.
  • Nigerian context by default, from LGA level up.
  • Every result is reproducible from the published code.
  • Models are tested against held-out data, and failures are reported, not hidden.
  • No synthetic data is presented as real; demonstration data is always labelled.

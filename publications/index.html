    Spatio-Temporal Analysis of Greenhouse Gases Concentration in Nigeria from 2019 to 2023

    Akintuyi, A. O., Wunude, E. O., & Akpan, P. P.

    Remote Sensing in Earth Systems Sciences, 9, Article 45 · Springer Nature · Published 20 June 2026

    A five-year, nationwide satellite analysis of carbon monoxide (CO), methane (CH₄), nitrogen dioxide (NO₂), ozone (O₃) and sulphur dioxide (SO₂) over Nigeria, using Sentinel-5P TROPOMI observations processed in Google Earth Engine to map their spatial patterns and track how concentrations changed between 2019 and 2023.

    Developed from my BSc Geography thesis at the University of Lagos (2024), supervised by Dr. Akinlabi Akintuyi, in which I carried out the Sentinel-5P processing in Google Earth Engine, the mapping and the trend analysis.
